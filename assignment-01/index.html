My Top 5 Video Games

A personal list of my favorite video games that have left a lasting impact over the years. After about 25 years of gaming, these are the games I always find myself enjoying the most and coming back to time and time again.

Back to Homepage

  1. World of Warcraft

    A massive multiplayer online role-playing game (MMORPG) that has captivated millions of players worldwide with its expansive world and engaging gameplay. Gamers can explore Azeroth and other worlds, complete quests, conquer difficult bosses and raids, collect achievments, mounts, pets and transmogs, and build lasting friendhips with other players worldwide.

  2. Sea of Thieves

    A fun and cooperative pirate adventure game that allows players to freely sail on the high seas, engage in ship battles, search for buried treasure, battle skeleton fleets, and create unique stores with their crew. The graphics for the water in this game are beautiful but the gameplay is equally engaging.

  3. Call of Duty Franchise

    A fast-paced, popular first-person shooter (FPS) franchise known for its action-packed military campaigns, competitive multiplayer arenas, and highly addictive cooperative modes. The game has consistently delivered intense gameplay experiences.

  4. Minecraft

    A sandbox game made entirely of blocks, allowing players to build and explore vast worlds, gather resources, craft items, and engage in various adventures either solo or with friends. Its creative and survival modes offer endless possibilities for gameplay and imagination.

  5. The Sims Franchise

    A classic life simulation game that allows players to create and control virtual people, build homes, pursue careers, and experience various aspects of everyday life. The game offers a sandbox environment where players can experiment with different life choices and scenarios, providing endless entertainment and creativity.
